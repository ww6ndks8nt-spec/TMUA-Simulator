Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 October 2026

1. About this notice

1.1 This Privacy Notice explains how personal information is processed when you use ducktmua.com (“the Website”). References to “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the operator of DuckTMUA.

1.2 You may contact us at duck79330@gmail.com regarding privacy, personal information, paper removal requests and other inquiries.

2. Information we process

2.1 Account information includes your email address, chosen display name, account identifier, verification status and account timestamps. Credentials are handled through Google Firebase Authentication. Your plaintext password is not stored in DuckTMUA’s practice database. An authentication record is created before you verify your email address; verification is required to access protected practice features.

2.2 Practice information includes selected answers, scores, attempt dates, question timings, unfinished attempts, bookmarks, mistake-journal entries, study plans, sessions and preferences.

2.3 Local copies of progress and settings are stored in your browser. Uploaded profile pictures and mental maths preferences are stored locally and are not included in cloud synchronisation. Mental maths session statistics are calculated in your browser.

2.4 We record your leaderboard choice, its date, the source of the choice and the notice version to manage and demonstrate your consent. A choice made during registration may be stored locally until email verification and synchronisation complete.

2.5 Hosting, authentication, content-delivery and security providers process technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device details, request timestamps and security signals when providing their services.

2.6 If you contact us, we process your email address, message and any attachments or information you provide. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Purposes and legal bases

3.1 Account information and saved practice data are used to provide the account, synchronisation and study services you request. Where applicable, this processing is necessary to perform our agreement with you or take steps at your request before entering that agreement. Account details are necessary for account-based features; leaderboard participation is optional.

3.2 Necessary technical information is processed to operate the Website, investigate faults and prevent abuse, based on our legitimate interest in maintaining a secure and reliable educational service, subject to users’ rights and interests.

3.3 Future publication of eligible scores on the public leaderboard relies on your consent. You can opt in using an unticked checkbox when signing in or registering, or by enabling the option and saving in Settings. Existing default-on preferences do not count as a new opt-in.

3.4 We process correspondence to respond to inquiries and resolve problems, based on our legitimate interest in administering the Website. Information needed to comply with an applicable legal obligation is processed on that basis.

3.5 You may withdraw consent in Settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Reading this notice or accepting the Terms does not itself constitute consent to optional publication.

4. Public leaderboard information

4.1 After you opt in, eligible first-attempt scores are submitted automatically. Your saved choice applies across devices when synchronisation completes. Leaving the sign-in checkbox unticked does not revoke an existing choice; use Settings to withdraw it.

4.2 Public records contain a display name, score, paper, attempt timestamp, mode and account identifier. They do not contain your email address, password or full practice history.

4.3 Choosing “Anonymous” changes the displayed name but does not remove the underlying account identifier. Use the name-update setting to update existing entries.

4.4 Turn submission off and save, or use “Withdraw consent and remove public scores” in Settings, to stop future publication and request removal of existing entries. An internet connection is needed; retry if the site reports a failure. Private practice history is retained. Older public entries are not removed merely by installing this update; you can remove them using the same control or contact us. We cannot erase copies independently made by other people.

5. Service providers and disclosures

5.1 Google Firebase Authentication manages sign-in and Firebase Realtime Database stores synchronised records and leaderboards. Google reCAPTCHA assesses technical and interaction signals to help detect fraud and automated abuse.

5.2 GitHub Pages hosts the Website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Mathematical-rendering software is loaded through cdnjs, operated by Cloudflare; requesting it sends technical connection information to that provider. Account software is also loaded from Google’s content-delivery infrastructure.

5.3 Correspondence sent to our contact address is processed through Gmail. “Report an issue” prepares an email on your device; the report is sent only if you send it through your email service.

5.4 Information may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary and lawful to investigate abuse, respond to a legal claim or protect users. External websites opened through links have their own privacy arrangements.

6. Browser storage and cookies

6.1 Browser storage maintains sign-in sessions, progress, preferences and synchronisation. reCAPTCHA uses a security cookie when executed to support risk assessment.

6.2 You may clear the Website’s data through your browser settings. This can sign you out and erase preferences or unsynchronised progress. Signing out alone does not necessarily erase local copies. Clear site data after using a shared device.

7. International processing

7.1 Providers may process information outside your country and the European Economic Area. The practice database is configured in a European region, but Firebase Authentication operates from United States data centres.

7.2 Google, GitHub and Cloudflare describe their international-transfer arrangements in the provider documents linked below. These include participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers and contractual safeguards where applicable. The applicable arrangement depends on the provider, service and transfer. You may contact us for further information about the arrangements relevant to your data.

8. Retention and deletion

8.1 Account and synchronised practice information is normally retained while your account exists. The application does not automatically delete accounts solely because they are unverified or inactive.

8.2 Account deletion is available in Settings or by contacting us. Successful deletion removes the authentication account, synchronised profile and practice records, and associated leaderboard entries. Follow any on-screen instructions if deletion does not finish.

8.3 A limited record containing the account identifier and deletion flag is retained to prevent older devices from restoring deleted data. It contains no practice history. The current implementation has no automatic expiry for this record.

8.4 Local information may remain on other devices, and some preferences may remain after account deletion. Clear site data on each device to remove those copies. Downloaded backups remain under your control.

8.5 Provider logs and backups follow their applicable retention periods. Google states that authentication information is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after the associated deletion request.

8.6 Correspondence is kept as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the inquiry and related legal or administrative matters. Longer retention requires a specific lawful justification.

9. Your rights

9.1 Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or a portable copy of your information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9.2 Send requests to duck79330@gmail.com. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity and protect your account.

9.3 Requests are normally answered within one month. Where the law permits an extension due to complexity or the number of requests, we will explain it within that initial period.

10. Automated processing and security

10.1 Scores and study statistics are calculated automatically for educational guidance; they do not determine official examination results or admission. Automated security checks assess account activity and may restrict suspicious requests, depending on the security configuration. Contact us if legitimate access is incorrectly restricted.

10.2 Access controls and encrypted connections help protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes and contact

11.1 This notice may change to reflect the Website and its processing practices. The current version displays its update date, and material changes will be brought to users’ attention.

11.2 For privacy requests, paper removal requests and other inquiries, contact duck79330@gmail.com.
